What vitamins are in spinach?

10:24 AM
nutrition Nutrient Telemetry Breakdown

Spinach (Spinacia oleracea) is exceptionally nutrient-dense with high bio-potency. Below is the physiological breakdown of primary vitamins and cofactors per standard 100g raw serving:

Vitamin K1 483 mcg

>400% Daily Value. Critical cofactor for hepatic synthesis of prothrombin and osteocalcin mineralization.

Vitamin A (Beta-carotene) 469 mcg

52% DV. Precursor for rhodopsin synthesis and respiratory mucosal integrity maintenance.

Vitamin C 28 mg

31% DV. Potent water-soluble reducing agent; neutralizes reactive oxygen species and accelerates iron absorption.

Folate (Vitamin B9) 194 mcg

49% DV. Direct substrate for nucleotide biosynthesis, cellular remethylation, and erythrocyte maturation.

grain
Iron & Micronutrient Synergies Provides 2.7 mg non-heme iron (15% DV) and 99 mg calcium. Absorption efficiency is dynamically elevated when ingested alongside the co-occurring endogenous Vitamin C.
💡

Biochemical Optimization: Lightly steaming spinach denatures oxalic acid (reducing kidney burden) while incorporating dietary lipids (such as cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil) increases the micellar bioavailability of fat-soluble vitamins A and K up to 300%.
